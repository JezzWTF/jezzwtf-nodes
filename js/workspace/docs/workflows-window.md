# Workflows window

A floating panel, like Paste Stack: it never blocks the canvas, so you can keep working with it open.

## Opening and moving it

- Click **Workflows** in ComfyUI's top bar.
- Or press **Alt+Shift+W**, use the canvas right-click menu, or run "JezzWTF Workflows" from the command palette.
- Drag the title bar to move the panel and drag its corner to resize it. Its position, size and open state are remembered, including after a reload.
- **Esc** or the x button hides it, and the top bar button brings it back. Settings under JezzWTF can also show a floating launcher.

## What it shows

Your saved workflows and the pack's bundled examples, as cards with a schematic thumbnail, or as a list.

## Finding things

- **Search** looks at names, folders, models, prompts, notes, group titles and node types. Indexing runs in the background, so results improve for a moment after you open it.
- The sidebar has **All**, **Favourites**, **Recent**, **JezzWTF examples**, your folders, **What it makes** (video, image, upscale, inpaint, LoRA) and **Model** (Flux, Qwen, SDXL, MiniMax H3, Wan, LTX and others).
- **Sort** by recent, name or node count.

## Opening a workflow

Click a card to see its details, then press **Enter**, double-click, or use **Open**. Opening reuses a workflow's loaded tab so unsaved edits are kept. The panel stays open so you can keep browsing.

Favourites are stored in your browser.
